## Boundaries to remember

- The factory starts from an **already recorded** presenter video in `incoming/`.
- The Meta publisher is a separate workflow. Each video needs its own approval before posting.
- The factory and publisher schedules were inactive at the [[PROJECT#Transfer and next-chat instructions|last handoff]]. Confirm the current state before running anything.
- Keep API keys and tokens out of notes, chat, Git, and transfer archives.
